Geometry has provenance.

These are structural references alongside a separate classical squid Hodgkin–Huxley model. They are not a single fitted biological specimen. Changing view preserves the same electrical state; it does not change the model's resolution.

Experimental protein coordinates

  • 6J8J · human Naᵥ1.7 — cryo-EM, 3.2 Å, blocker/toxin-bound reference with auxiliary β subunits. 1,433 protein Cα positions shown; sidechains, glycans and nonprotein ligands omitted. Shen et al., Science (2019), publication.
  • 3LUT · rat Kᵥ1.2 — X-ray re-refinement, 2.9 Å. Full biological assembly: four α and four β subunits, 2,864 Cα positions. Ligands omitted. Chen et al., PNAS (2010), publication.

Backbones remain static. HH population activity changes illumination, not atomic coordinates. PDB coordinates are CC0 under the RCSB data policy.

Reconstructed morphology

Allen Institute for Brain Science, Allen Cell Types Database specimen 464212183, accessed October 11, 2026. This mouse dendrite-focused SWC contains 2,605 dendritic nodes, 14 axon-stub nodes and one soma. Fine branch radii are enlarged for visibility; branching topology comes from the reconstruction.

This Allen-derived asset follows the Allen Institute's research/noncommercial terms and citation policy. Its conditions are separate from the independent engine code and PDB data.
